# Welcome to the School Portal

This portal serves as the central hub for students, teachers, and visitors to access academic resources, administrative services, and school updates.

---

## 🎓 For Students
* **Academic Dashboard:** View enrolled courses, timetables, and assignment deadlines.
* **Grade Center:** Access report cards, continuous assessment scores, and academic transcripts.
* **Learning Resources:** Download lecture notes, study materials, and submit assignments online.
* **Attendance Tracking:** Monitor personal daily and course-based attendance records.

---

## 👩‍🏫 For Teachers
* **Class Management:** Manage student rosters, daily attendance, and subject schedules.
* **Grading & Assessment:** Upload marks, record continuous assessments, and generate report cards.
* **Instructional Delivery:** Share course materials, assignments, and announcements directly with students.
* **Performance Analytics:** Track and evaluate individual and class academic progress.

---

## 🌐 For Visitors
* **Admissions Information:** Learn about entry requirements, application procedures, and fee structures.
* **School News & Events:** Stay updated on upcoming school activities, academic calendars, and announcements.
* **About Us:** Explore the school’s history, mission, vision, and campus facilities.
* **Contact & Support:** Access official contact details, campus directions, and general inquiry forms.
